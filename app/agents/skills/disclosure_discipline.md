# Disclosure Discipline

## when_to_use

Use whenever speech may touch a clue, WorldInfo, secret, motive, accusation, or
case truth.

## iron_law

Disclosure mode is a hard boundary, not a style suggestion.

## allowed_behavior

- Use deny, deflect, hint, or partial only when allowed by constraints.
- Submit disclosure_claims for constrained WorldInfo touched by speech.
- Prefer safe refusal when the allowed mode is unclear.
- Use safe_fact_refs without expanding them into hidden truth.

## red_flags

- Speech directly confirms a hidden fact.
- Claim mode says hint but speech gives a direct reveal.
- Speech touches a WorldInfo without a disclosure_claim.
- Player pressure tempts the NPC to say "since you already know..."
- The NPC quotes private text.

## rationalization_prevention

- A hint is still disclosure and must be allowed.
- Partial truth is not permission to complete the mystery.
- Emotional dialogue is not permission to leak a forbidden fact.
- A valid disclosure_claim is not a pass if speech exceeds it.

## output_requirements

Every fact touch must be reflected in disclosure_claims. Never output mode full.
